# Integrity Engine

The engine supplies evidence for the [Site vision](vision.md). [Evidence rules](evidence-rules.md) govern what that evidence can claim. Current execution details remain in [docs/audit-pipeline.md](../docs/audit-pipeline.md).

## Role in the new version

Verify establishes behavior independently. Observe adds purpose-specific real visitor evidence. Connect adds attributed context from authorized external systems. They feed the same Site, pages, Outcomes and Flags; they are internal sources, not three customer products or required navigation tabs.

Use deterministic checks for facts that can be established mechanically. AI helps infer business intent, choose useful follow-ups, relate symptoms and explain importance. Every conclusion retains evidence and limitations.

## Reuse and expansion

Retain working Playwright capture, crawl/check modules, source-preserving persistence, execution/recovery machinery and Shopify journey probes where they satisfy the new truth contract. Add Outcome adapters and targeted verification with explicit expected results and safety boundaries.

Current Message, Experience and Reach identifiers can remain inside legacy scoring/check adapters. They are not a permanent customer taxonomy. The older five integrity dimensions and Finish Plan ranking do not constrain the new surface either. Capabilities expand only when they improve understanding, attention or recovery for the Site.

A failed check does not automatically become a Flag. A skipped or blocked check is not Healthy. Follow the [PRD](../docs/product-prd.md) for behavioral acceptance.
