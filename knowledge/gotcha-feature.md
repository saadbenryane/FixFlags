## 1. The Gotcha Feature

### 1.1 Solve your own problem

Build something you are personally passionate about. First-hand conviction resonates with your audience and makes it possible to convince influencers and communities of your mission.

### 1.2 The 5-Second Rule

A successful gotcha feature must be so simple that anyone looking at it for 3-5 seconds instantly understands what the app does without further explanation.

Spend ~90% of your time on this one core feature. It is the primary engine for both product value and viral distribution.

| App | Gotcha feature | One-line thesis |
|-----|---------------|-----------------|
| CalAI | Take a picture of food → instant calorie count | "Point at food, get calories" |
| Wrestle AI | Upload match video → technical feedback | "Upload a match, get a scout report" |
| Uber | Request ride → car picks you up | "Tap a button, a car arrives" |
| DoorDash | Order food → delivered | "Browse restaurants, food arrives" |

### 1.3 Reverse-engineer virality

Design the gotcha feature by reverse-engineering what would make a viral TikTok or Instagram Reel. Build the product around a single, highly shareable "stop the scroll" moment.

The shareable moment *is* the marketing. If the core action is not inherently shareable in under 5 seconds, the product thesis is wrong.

### 1.4 Niche over mass appeal

AI democratizes creation. You can build highly profitable apps for small, specific communities (e.g., wrestlers) that do not need to be billion-dollar companies to succeed. A narrow, passionate audience beats a broad, indifferent one.

## 2. Product Design & Onboarding

### 2.1 The Mom Test

If your mom cannot figure out how the app functions without your help, the UI is too cluttered and must be simplified.

### 2.2 Onboarding as a sales funnel

Onboarding is not a tutorial — it is a sales funnel designed to prime the user for conversion.

| Function | Purpose |
|----------|---------|
| **Education** | Explain what the product is and why the user needs it. No jargon. |
| **Social proof** (optional) | Show that others use and trust the app. |
| **Personalization** (sunken cost) | Ask 20+ specific questions (e.g., "What weight do you wrestle at?"). Users invest time upfront and become more likely to accept a paywall. |
| **FOMO** | Trigger fear of missing out just before the paywall. |

### 2.3 FOMO pattern

1. User performs a core action (e.g., uploads a match video)
2. App shows mock analysis animation: "calibrating...", "targeting..."
3. Paywall appears: "Unlock your rating"
4. User has invested in the upload and watched the animation — peak curiosity, higher conversion

The paywall interrupts at peak curiosity, not at first touch.

### 2.4 Retention through utility

The viral feature brings users in. Adjacent useful features (e.g., calorie tracking for wrestlers) keep them subscribed and reduce churn.

## 3. Vibe Coding & Development

### 3.1 No-code technicality

You can build high-quality, scalable consumer apps in Swift using AI tools (Cursor, etc.) without knowing how to code.

### 3.2 Prompt engineering vs. AI slop

Success comes from manually tweaking and consistently prompting the AI over ~14 days of iteration — not from trying to "one-shot" the entire app.

### 3.3 Focus on consumer apps

For standard consumer tools, vibe-coded apps do not currently face major scalability or security issues because they often avoid collecting sensitive personal data.

## 4. Distribution & Sales

### 4.1 Social proof first

Build a professional-looking Instagram page with clean product demos. Consider buying followers or verification to establish authority with influencers.

### 4.2 The numbers game

Distribution is high-volume. Tailor your "For You Page" to your Ideal Customer Profile (ICP) to find influencers, then DM hundreds of them daily.

### 4.3 Social skills as a superpower

Closing deals is ~90% sales. Build rapport through shared interests (the "homie discount") to negotiate lower rates or equity partnerships.

### 4.4 The VA framework

Hire a Virtual Assistant (VA) as soon as possible to handle manual scrolling and outreach. This frees you for higher-level strategy.

### 4.5 Scaling with paid ads

Once you have 5-15 quality creatives from influencers, use them for Meta Ads. Study the Meta Ads Library to replicate what works for competitors.
