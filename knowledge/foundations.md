# Foundations

The [complete owner vision](vision.md) is the authority. These principles guide implementation tradeoffs.

- Care compounds around a permanent Site, its purpose and history.
- Outcomes express what visitors must be able to do; page count and scores are secondary.
- Checks supply evidence; Flags earn attention. Zero important Flags can be success.
- Coverage and freshness make health statements meaningful.
- Independent verification closes the loop. A submitted fix is not proof of recovery.
- Complexity belongs underneath a small, mobile-first interface.
- Connections improve existing answers and keep one coherent product.
- Quiet healthy days and rare useful alerts are product value.
- Privacy, tenant isolation and minimal collection are foundations of trust.
- Reuse the brand and sound infrastructure while replacing the report experience.

The original AI-generation thesis remains historical context, not a limit on who can use FixFlags. The customer can run a store, SaaS site, local business, nonprofit, or another website with important Outcomes.
