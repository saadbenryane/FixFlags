## 2. Reddit r/vibecoding Post

### Title
I ran FixFlags on 10 Lovable apps. Here is what kept breaking.

### Body
I built FixFlags to check AI-built sites before you share the link. To test it, I ran it on 10 real apps built with Lovable, Bolt, and Cursor.

Here is what kept showing up:

**1. CTA hidden below the fold on mobile (7 out of 10 apps)**
The hero image looks great on desktop. On a 375px phone, it pushes the signup button below the first screen. Most visitors never scroll that far.

**2. Missing og:image (6 out of 10)**
Every time someone shares your link on Slack, X, or WhatsApp, it shows a blank card. Takes 2 minutes to fix. Almost nobody does it.

**4. Headline says what the product is, not what it does for you (5 out of 10)**
"AI-powered task management for teams" tells me nothing. "Ship projects 2x faster without the status meetings" tells me why I care.

**5. No privacy policy link (5 out of 10)**
Especially if you are collecting emails. People will not sign up without it.

**6. Navigation menu takes half the mobile screen (4 out of 10)**
Announcement bar + nav + hamburger = 280px before any content. On a phone, that is a dealbreaker.

**The interesting part:** I fixed 3 of these on my own site, re-checked, and the flags cleared. The re-check loop actually works.

If you want to try it: fixflags.com. Free check, no account needed for the teaser. Fix prompts are shaped for Cursor, Lovable, Bolt, and Claude Code.

What issues do you keep finding on your own AI-built apps?

### Posting strategy
- Post Tuesday or Wednesday between 9-11 AM EST
- Do NOT link to FixFlags in the title
- Include 1-2 screenshots of actual flags (with evidence)
- Reply to every comment within the first 2 hours
- Follow up 3 days later with a "here is what I learned from 50 scans" post
