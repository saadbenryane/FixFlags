## App introduction

FixFlags walks your product page to checkout on a phone-sized browser. If the path breaks, you get the video and an alert.

Store up is not the same as can buy. Uptime checks miss a dead add to cart. FixFlags confirms Can't buy twice before it emails you, then tells you when the path can take orders again.

We stop before payment. We do not record real shoppers. We do not invent conversion percentages.

## Features

- Auto-discover up to two buyable purchase paths
- Mobile walk: product, add to cart, cart, checkout
- Video of the walk FixFlags ran, with GIF and screenshot fallback
- Email on confirmed Can't buy and recovery; optional Slack
- Recheck after you fix a theme or app (five per day on Free)
- Improve stays off the alarm
