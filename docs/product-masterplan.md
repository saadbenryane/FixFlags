# FixFlags completion plan

FixFlags must take an ordinary website URL through discovery, meaningful Flags, useful repair, independent verification and automatic continuing monitoring. The owner should understand what is watched, what needs attention, what to do next and whether the fix worked.

The ordinary-owner loop now passes on compiled candidate `96a9fa5`, including the corrected worker continuation runtime. Subsequent subscription discovery and destination-content dependency changes pass four native repair cycles on `98eeb873`, with their original deadlines. Full product completion remains open. The largest remaining work is to reconcile native evidence for every advertised responsibility, resolve reliability concerns, finish actual provider and unattended-operation acceptance, and validate the complete experience with people. This document is the single active execution plan. The [preserved November 9 snapshot](product-masterplan-history-2026-11-09.md) retains previous implementation details, research, failures and receipts with their original limitations.

## Current scope and completion standard

Complete the existing website product: URL entry, same-Site claim, all eight coverage areas, page and category depth, individual and grouped repair, comparable verification, recurrence, default HTTP uptime, scheduled analysis, notifications, authorized account checks, settings, revocation and acknowledged Site removal. Preserve existing commercial behavior, subscriptions, credits, saved connections, retained reports and accepted detection semantics. The actual catalog remains authoritative when its inventory changes.

Use the existing modular monolith, progressive execution, evidence receipts and Improvement ledger. Correct a foundational defect where the fact or effect is owned, migrate its callers and remove the superseded current path. Keep required historical compatibility. Refactoring must improve a demonstrated correctness, performance or maintenance problem. Do not add another execution engine, acceptance registry or customer dashboard.

Marketing and branding remain outside this work. Use their advertised capabilities as requirements. Paid enrollment, public MCP/CLI discovery, repository execution and unvalidated connections retain separate gates. Existing authorized API and machine-client consumers still require compatible shared facts. Backups and restore remain outside the current scope under the owner's November 6 direction. No deployment or new external effect is authorized by this plan.

Completion requires meaningful functional evidence, trustworthy shared facts, coherent implementation, a minimal usable interface and representative integrated app/database/queue/worker behavior. Local observations, real-provider effects, natural scheduling, human usability and deployed identity retain separate acceptance.
