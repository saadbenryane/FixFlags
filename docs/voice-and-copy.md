# Voice and copy

Target language follows the [September 8 vision](../knowledge/vision.md). [SOUL.md](../SOUL.md) owns personality. Runtime marketing copy lives in lib/marketing/copy.ts and its modules.

## Message and actions

The target headline is **Your website, looked after.**

The promise is **FixFlags tells you how your website is doing, what needs attention, and keeps watching.**

Primary URL action: **Check my website**. After useful value: **Keep watching**. On actionable Flags: **Fix this** and **Verify fix**. Outcome confirmation: **Looks right · Edit**.

Use Site, Outcome, Flag, Coverage and Connection. The interface contract lives in [workspace-interface.md](workspace-interface.md). Internal Verify / Observe / Connect architecture does not need to be explained before the customer receives value.

## Sentence rules

Use familiar words, concrete behavior and active sentences. Lead with the human effect, then offer technical evidence. No em dashes anywhere in newly authored customer copy. Avoid filler such as comprehensive, seamless, robust, game-changing, revolutionary, unlock and leverage.

Do not ban useful factual words such as “broken” when a real failure warrants them. Avoid patronizing reassurance, fake urgency, fear about AI tools and unsubstantiated loss claims. Short copy must still communicate uncertainty and scope.

## Truth in language

Confirmed, Likely, Couldn't verify and Healthy follow [evidence rules](../knowledge/evidence-rules.md). A live server is not a healthy purchase flow. “Confirmed twice” needs real repeated evidence. “Resolved” needs fresh independent verification of the relevant behavior. A deployment-time correlation must not become an asserted cause.

“Everything important we're watching is healthy” requires current relevant coverage. “You're covered” requires ongoing checking to be active. A saved account alone is insufficient. Never invent coverage counts, customer examples, testimonials, alert delivery or connected capabilities.

## Progressive depth

First explain the customer problem. Then expose scope and business context. Then reproduction, requests, screenshots and implementation guidance. The same Flag serves owner, marketer, developer and agent. Avoid building separate voices or products for each.

## Marketing transition

“Finish what your AI started”, report-first promotion, Shopify-only company positioning and separate website/funnel product language are retired for the next version. Shopify can have a tailored native entry and page as “FixFlags for Shopify”, serving the same product.

The homepage, how-it-works, pricing, navigation, metadata, social previews, samples, public docs/help, emails and Shopify listing must be reconciled as their corresponding behavior ships. The new headline cannot be used to imply monitoring is active before it exists. Use real Site/Flag evidence for proof. Old runtime docs remain accurate for compatibility until cutover; this guide is not a statement that the public surfaces are already updated.
