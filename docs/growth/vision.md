# Vision

## What we're building

FixFlags becomes **the most authoritative destination on the internet for
quality assurance of AI-built products** — and organic acquisition compounds
as a byproduct of that authority, not as a separate marketing effort.

## The loop

```
Product generates knowledge
  → Knowledge generates public assets
    → Assets generate trust
      → Trust generates organic acquisition
        → Acquisition generates more audits
          → Audits generate more knowledge (back to top)
```

Every audit FixFlags runs teaches it something about how AI-built products
fail. That knowledge is currently thrown away after each report is delivered.
This system captures it, aggregates it, and turns it into public assets that
only FixFlags could produce — because only FixFlags has run the audits.

## What "organic growth" means here

It does **not** mean:
- publishing more pages
- chasing keyword volume
- vanity traffic
- isolated SEO metrics (rankings without conversions)

It **does** mean:
- a knowledge graph that grows with every audit
- public pages that exist because they carry information only we have
- a measurement loop that tells us which improvements actually moved
  acquisition, conversion, or revenue
- documentation that makes every future iteration smarter than the last

## Scope

**In scope for the growth system:**
- Technical SEO (sitemap, robots, structured data, crawl health)
- Information architecture for public pages
- The internal knowledge graph and its rollup jobs
- Public reports, benchmarks, issue library, comparisons, free tools
- Content strategy grounded in graph data (not generic AI content)
- Experimentation and measurement infrastructure
- This documentation workspace

**Out of scope (for now, revisit when the above is mature):**
- Paid acquisition (ads, sponsorships) — a different system, different owner
- Outbound sales / partnerships
- Community building (Discord, forums) — evaluate once organic has traction
- Backlink outreach campaigns — premature before we have link-worthy assets

## Non-goals

- We will not publish a page just because it targets a keyword.
- We will not fabricate statistics, member counts, or testimonials (see
  `AGENTS.md` — this rule already exists at the marketing-copy level and
  extends to every growth surface).
- We will not chase index-count vanity metrics. A shrinking, higher-quality
  index beats a bloated one.
- We will not treat this as a marketing sprint. It's a permanent system with
  its own architecture, backlog, and review cadence — see `growth-roadmap.md`.

## Success, ultimately

FixFlags is the site people link to when they want to say "here's proof that
AI-built products have a systemic QA gap" — and the site people run their own
audits on when they want to close it.
