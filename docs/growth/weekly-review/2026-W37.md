### This cycle's intervention

- **Stable ID:** `SEO-CRAWL-001`
- **Target query/page:** `/sitemap.xml`, `/issues`, and related links on
  `/issues/[checkId]`
- **Audience and intent:** people and crawlers discovering the public Flag
  Library
- **Dated baseline:** 19 invalid `::page:N` sitemap URLs and three duplicate
  related links on the sampled `no-structured-data` issue page at
  2026-09-08T17:21Z
- **Exact change:** exclude page-scoped check variants from public issue
  discovery, deduplicate related issues by base check ID, and preserve only
  valid sample-gated canonical issue routes
- **Expected causal mechanism:** eliminate known Not Found discovery paths
  and make crawl/navigation signals coherent
- **Primary search metric:** zero `::page:N` entries in the production
  sitemap after deployment
- **Product/business guardrail:** canonical eligible issue pages and up to
  three distinct related issue links remain available
- **Observation window and next measurement date:** immediate production
  verification after deployment; first GSC review on 2026-09-15 or after a
  confirmed recrawl
- **Keep/revise/revert rule:** keep if invalid entries reach zero without
  losing eligible issues; revise if another public projection leaks variants;
  revert if canonical eligible issue coverage disappears
- **Why this beat the strongest alternative:** it is directly observed,
  locally fixable, user-visible, and does not depend on hidden query intent
  or infrastructure hostname ownership
