## Verified preparation retained

The reusable website base is URL analysis → persistent Site → evidence-backed Flags → fix handoff → fresh verification, with separately configured monitoring. Next.js, Prisma/PostgreSQL, Redis/BullMQ and the dedicated Playwright worker already support it. Project backs Site, Audit records physical execution, SiteOutcome binds responsibility, and Improvement supplies stable Flag identity. `lib/sites/application/run-requests.ts` owns the shared tenant-scoped run path. [PRODUCT.md](../PRODUCT.md) owns the full inventory; [release evidence](release-evidence-runbook.md) owns production proof.

Availability, Checkout and authorized Safe Signup have workspace execution contracts. Checkout verifies cart contents/checkout opening, not a paid order. Signup requires a reversible authorized fixture; login and password reset are unavailable. Broad diagnostics do not establish server-side root cause or blanket Outcome health. Shopify/Analytics/Search Console add context. Public GitHub is sign-in and Flag handoff; external MCP/client launch proof remains separate.

Read-only live homepage inspection at 1440 × 1000 and 393 × 852, reduced motion: clear URL entry, shared taxonomy and useful sample recovery story; no document overflow; mobile depth scrolled and Escape restored focus after dismissal. Verified defects:

- Category/status overlap in approximately 157px-wide mobile cards.
- Clear `.answer` text is 22px versus 17px Flag lists. Desktop rows measured 248px/152px; mobile rows varied 258px/204px/175px. Content/media drive the heights; equal height alone is not the solution.
- Pages depicts a store thumbnail for a pricing-page 404; Performance mixes campaign-page copy with homepage LCP facts; Conversion mixes Signup and Checkout failures with cart-only evidence. Sources: `BoardCard.module.css`, `lib/marketing/copy/care-homepage.ts` and `CareHomepage.tsx` depth branches.
- Marketing implies ongoing care sooner than onboarding establishes it. Provisional Sites initialize without a Watch schedule; minute-relative sample alerts are not a detection SLA.

Narrative repetition, small comparison cells and the oversized integration diagram were editorial concerns, not measured conversion failures. Authenticated onboarding, scheduled delivery, real recovery and production/client parity were not exercised. The strategic review below replaces the earlier card-polish-only recommendation.

## Research that changes the decision

Primary sources accessed October 10, 2026. Facts below describe public positioning, documentation and demonstrations, not authenticated hands-on evaluations or independently proven vendor outcomes. Lessons and tradeoffs are our interpretation. Rendered Checkly, v0 and Linear entry pages were inspected; Lovable blocked the browser with a security challenge, so its visual experience was not evaluated. Exact prices are omitted where plan data could not be established.

### Monitoring and website intelligence

- **Checkly is the closest strategic competitor.** Its [homepage](https://www.checklyhq.com/) presents an active reliability layer for developers and agents, with an agent → check code → deployment → regional results demonstration. Its [quickstart](https://www.checklyhq.com/docs/quickstart/) lets a coding agent discover targets, generate tests and deploy after confirmation. Its [MCP](https://www.checklyhq.com/docs/ai/mcp-server/) reads evidence, triggers checks and supports account actions/RCA. Its [free plan](https://www.checklyhq.com/pricing/) includes 1,000 browser runs monthly. **Lesson:** demonstrate the entire working path, and make setup easy. **Tradeoff/opportunity:** code-controlled monitoring is a strength for engineering teams; test whether FixFlags' URL-first broader understanding is preferable for customers who do not want to own a monitor codebase. Do not claim that competitors require manual scripting or lack independent rechecks.
- **Datadog serves an integrated observability buyer.** [Browser-test documentation](https://docs.datadoghq.com/synthetics/browser_tests/) describes templates, recording, devices, locations and configurable cadence. Its [platform navigation](https://www.datadoghq.com/product/synthetic-monitoring/) includes AI investigation and MCP; [pricing](https://www.datadoghq.com/pricing/) is organized by products, with a trial entry. **Lesson:** credibility comes from results and investigation context, not checkmarks. **Tradeoff:** powerful configuration and infrastructure context suit engineering operations. FixFlags should not mimic the platform menu or imply that external inspection provides Datadog's server-side visibility. The browser-test markdown was fetched directly after the web tool rejected its content type; no full price audit was completed.
- **Better Stack sells an operational system.** Its [homepage](https://betterstack.com/) connects AI SRE, telemetry, incidents, Playwright transactions and MCP, with a concrete migration/cost story. Its [free offer](https://betterstack.com/pricing/) includes monitors and email/Slack alerts. **Lesson:** demonstrate what happens after detection and keep humans in control. **Tradeoff:** on-call and observability context are meaningful strengths, not clutter by definition. Avoid copying replacement/savings claims or paging expectations FixFlags cannot prove.
- **DebugBear makes diagnosis tangible.** Its [product page](https://www.debugbear.com/) shows lab/field data, waterfalls, before/after results and coding-agent prompts. [Pricing](https://www.debugbear.com/pricing), inspected in a browser because it requires JavaScript, offers a 14-day no-card trial and budgets synthetic tests/RUM. **Lesson:** show contributing evidence and a useful fix, not just a score. **Tradeoff:** specialist performance depth is a reason to keep it. Do not imply that broad coverage makes FixFlags a superior performance analyzer or that AI fix prompts are unique.
- **Ghost Inspector removes test authoring's code barrier.** Its [homepage](https://ghostinspector.com/) demonstrates recording/editing browser tests, visual/accessibility testing and scheduling for QA, engineers and product managers. Its [trial](https://ghostinspector.com/pricing/) exposes Scale features for 14 days; run volume is bounded. **Lesson:** full capability exploration and limited consumption can coexist. **Tradeoff:** recording gives users explicit control over expected behavior. Do not promise that inference eliminates this control or safely handles every protected action.
- **Semrush owns search improvement.** Its [URL-first SEO checker](https://www.semrush.com/siteaudit/) shows a health snapshot, passed/attention results and prioritized guidance, leading into recurring Site Audit. Its [plans](https://www.semrush.com/pricing/seo-ai-search/) combine SEO/AI search, limits, free entry and trial. **Lesson:** breadth is understandable when organized around one customer job. **Tradeoff:** discovery and visibility are not purchase-flow proof. Avoid imitating a large tool catalog or implying that technical crawlability guarantees rankings.
- **Siteimprove competes on breadth and governance.** Its [homepage](https://www.siteimprove.com/) unifies accessibility, search, analytics and content with agents, code suggestions and MCP announcements. It offers a URL report/checkers and demo-led enterprise access; the attempted pricing URL redirects home, so no price comparison is established. **Lesson:** broad diagnostics and agents already share a product story elsewhere. **Tradeoff:** compliance-oriented depth and enterprise governance are valuable. FixFlags should not suggest automated accessibility coverage certifies compliance.
- **Hexometer's current entry is different from older descriptions.** [hexometer.com](https://hexometer.com/) now presents change monitoring and points to [OR:ON](https://orion.hexowatch.com/). Browser inspection of OR:ON showed URL → plain-language condition → explanatory before/after alerts, history, MCP, and two daily Watches on Free. **Lesson:** a monitor can be understandable through a specific change and meaningful quietness. **Tradeoff:** change notification is not functional recovery proof. Do not repeat the historical six-area Hexometer positioning as a confirmed current fact or copy its bot-bypass promise.
- **PageSpeed Insights sets the baseline for free first use.** [Google's documentation](https://developers.google.com/speed/docs/insights/v5/about) distinguishes Lighthouse lab diagnostics from CrUX field data and missing samples; [entry](https://pagespeed.web.dev/) begins with a URL. **Lesson:** valuable technical breadth can be freely explorable, and uncertainty can be precise. **Tradeoff:** a page measurement is not recurring customer-journey responsibility. Avoid reducing FixFlags to a score or treating field data as a fresh synthetic test.

### Frontier product references

- **Codex:** [official quickstart](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/quickstart) puts a task in a chosen workspace/environment; [permissions](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/agent-approvals-security) distinguish what the agent can do from what needs approval. **Learn:** concrete work context and reviewable results make delegation understandable. **Avoid:** transplanting a coding agent's write permissions or model controls into a monitor. This reference used official documentation, not a completed Codex marketing/pricing evaluation.
- **Cursor:** [homepage](https://cursor.com/) demonstrates coding tasks, autonomous work and results ready for review; [Free](https://cursor.com/pricing) limits Agent requests. **Learn:** give the sophisticated product a recognizable role and show completed work in its real interface. **Avoid:** implying autonomy means unlimited authority or that an agent's own test proves independent recovery.
- **Claude Code:** [overview](https://code.claude.com/docs/en/overview) makes code reading, editing, commands, diffs and MCP concrete across existing work surfaces; most surfaces require a subscription or API account. **Learn:** fit into the customer's existing workflow and explain capabilities through tasks. **Avoid:** treating terminal setup or an always-visible chat as necessary for website operators.
- **Lovable:** [homepage](https://lovable.dev/) moves from an idea through live preview/refinement to deployment and infrastructure; [free credits](https://lovable.dev/pricing) bound actual creation. **Learn:** direct action makes sophistication approachable, then the result reveals breadth. **Avoid:** borrowing creation promises, implying payment capability from a demo, or copying visual styling without a rendered evaluation.
- **v0:** [entry](https://v0.app/) leads with a composer and discoverable templates, then publishing, repo sync and design controls. [Free](https://v0.app/pricing) includes meaningful build/deploy functions with a daily message cap. **Learn:** useful depth need not compete with the primary action. **Avoid:** a prompt-first interface when a URL already supplies the right initial context.
- **Replit:** [homepage](https://replit.com/) combines example tasks, creation, publication and infrastructure, with free daily usage and one published project. **Learn:** first-use output and the ongoing relationship belong in one story. **Avoid:** implying FixFlags builds, hosts or repairs the customer's application.
- **Devin:** [homepage](https://devin.ai/) demonstrates task → implementation → browser recording → PR review and recurring automation; [Free](https://devin.ai/pricing) has a light agent allowance. **Learn:** show deliverables and the review boundary. **Avoid:** the autonomous-engineer promise; fixing software is outside FixFlags' current role. Browser testing is not ours alone.
- **Manus:** [homepage](https://manus.im/) presents recognizable deliverables rather than model taxonomy: slides, websites, games and other tasks. **Learn:** make the first action obvious and let examples show scope. **Avoid:** a general-purpose assistant promise that hides responsibility and evidence. Its pricing page did not reveal reliable plan details through web or browser inspection; no free allowance is asserted.
- **Linear:** [homepage](https://linear.app/) shows a substantial working interface and organizes its story around intake, planning, agents and review. [Free](https://linear.app/pricing) supports a real workspace with a bounded issue count. **Learn:** information organization creates perceived quality; density and restraint can coexist. **Avoid:** copying its dark aesthetic or adding daily engagement rituals to background infrastructure.

**Conclusion:** AI, breadth, MCP, browser evidence and rechecking all overlap with existing products. The opportunity is a coherent, low-effort website relationship with trustworthy coverage and recovery. That is a product-experience hypothesis, not an established moat. Compare actual customer setup and interpretation effort, rather than inventing feature absence.

## Three positioning territories, for review

Judgments below are strategic assessments, not customer research scores.

| Criterion | Website health with evidence | Independent verification for shipping | An AI caretaker for your website |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Customer clarity | Familiar website job; explain scope | Clear to builders; more abstract to operators | Familiar agent idea, ambiguous responsibility |
| Differentiation | Integrated experience must earn it | Strong mechanism; Checkly overlaps directly | Weak label; many monitoring agents exist |
| Emotional appeal | Competence and control | Momentum with justified confidence | Delegation and reassurance |
| Product credibility | Broad diagnostics and bounded monitoring fit | Independent engine fits; deployment/client proof still needed | Risks implying repair and unattended protected actions |
| AI-building relevance | Useful regardless of creation tool | Direct response to faster changes | Overt AI but little extra customer meaning |
| Commercial potential | Recurring care if accurate/useful | Budget tied to releases; may become episodic | Hard to price without measurable responsibility |
| Expansion | Website market first, adaptable architecture | Best bridge to API/agent Outcomes | Broad but potentially vague |

**Recommend website health with evidence.** Use a familiar category to explain the job and independent verification to earn trust. Initially test with small teams operating commercial websites and shipping frequent changes, especially stores with a supported purchase path. This is a fit hypothesis based on Checkout/Shopify foundations, not proven demand. Agencies are a useful second validation group; broad AI-builder distribution should follow exact-client proof. Preserve other customers' usefulness and the wider architecture.

- **Positioning statement:** For teams responsible for a live website, FixFlags brings website analysis, important customer journeys and recurring checks into one clear experience, with evidence that carries from a problem through fixing and fresh verification.
- **Customer category:** Website health monitoring.
- **Proposed headline:** See what works. Know what needs fixing.
- **Supporting copy:** A clear view of your website, from pages and performance to the journeys people rely on. Explore the evidence, fix what matters, and choose what FixFlags keeps watching.
- **CTA:** Analyze your website, using the existing Analyze action; secondary: Explore the sample.
- **Primary reason to believe:** a traceable observation of the same behavior failing and then succeeding in a fresh independent execution, with wider checked scope visible. Show a labeled fixture until genuine publishable customer proof exists.
- **Main objection:** Is this another audit that creates work without dependable ongoing value?

The existing broader brand line may remain an umbrella. It should not be required to explain the initial website product. AI explains how parts work; it is not the reason a customer must care. Do not adopt the proposed words until reviewed. Test comprehension and interest separately from uptake: can people accurately explain scope, evidence and monitoring after seeing it, and will they activate it on a real Site?

## Complete product, organized by depth

**Overview:** one Site, current answer, checked scope/freshness, priority attention and a full supported category board. Keep every evidenced area available, including calm success. Represent unchecked supported areas in coverage rather than silently disappearing them. Show monitoring off/on/paused, scope and next execution; individual categories retain their own checked times. One Site-wide date must not imply every tile was just checked.

**Category:** summarize what the area answers, then expose all available successful receipts, Flags, Recommendations, incomplete/blocked/not-applicable results and uncovered scope, grouped by page or behavior. Prioritize and paginate many failures with honest totals and filters; do not cap meaningful findings to one. Unsupported is different from supported-but-unchecked. Do not infer passed tests from absence of Flags or invent per-check receipts from module-level evidence.

**Flag:** observed versus expected behavior, affected scope, evidence and certainty, possible contributing factors, fix guidance and independent verification history. A category's Checkout and Signup failures need separate scope and proof. Copying a prompt is a handoff, not remediation. The product should suggest useful safe scope before asking users to author a journey; protected actions still require authorized fixtures.

**Monitoring:** an ongoing responsibility, not a fourth task customers must memorize. Quiet success is useful when customers can see what ran and what runs next. Notify meaningful failures, verified recovery, and loss of promised coverage; not every scan completion. Store or web-app priorities may reorder real evidence and suggest setup, but must not fabricate covered behavior from a detected site type.

Daily verification leaves potentially day-long detection gaps. Rapid outage protection would require a separately implemented, costed and proven pulse; do not use real-time incident promises to sell the present cadence.

The current architecture supports shared BoardCard, responsive depth, category projections, module receipts, stable Flags, binding assessments, history, monitoring state and one run path. Still incomplete/unproven: uniformly explorable per-check results, automatic safe coverage activation, consistent sample scope, mobile card anatomy, authenticated first-use proof, production monitoring/client reliability and validated retention. `readCoverageExecutions()` projects named module scopes, not a complete ledger for every advertised test. Build missing presentation/persistence deliberately when the data cannot answer the question.

## Temporary free validation policy proposal

**Founder intent is approved; these operating numbers are not.** Expose supported capabilities, bound consumption, keep the paid waitlist, and leave Stripe and existing subscriber records untouched. Use a time-bounded early-access grant in the existing entitlement layer, not a pretend paid subscription or a global bypass.

Suggested initial grant: **one Site, 60 days**, renewable explicitly during validation; **10 full analyses per rolling 30 days** with all existing analysis depths available (up to depth 3), bounded by current safety/deadline ceilings; **one daily scheduled run across up to three bound behaviors**; **20 fresh targeted verifications per 30 days**, maximum three interactive executions per day, with one active run per Site and reuse/idempotency. These are provisional budgets, not cost-validated quotas. Preserve current internal page/deadline ceilings and disclose the actual captured page scope. Additional agency Sites require a separately budgeted pilot, not fake shared identities. Do not include unsupported repo scanning, login, password reset or unreleased generalized agent evaluations under “full product.”

One anonymous analysis may expose all supported areas within its explicit bounded scope, all observed results and public-safe guidance for the private provisional session. Retain cookie/IP/host abuse controls. Account creation earns durable ownership, history, recurring notification configuration and private connection access; it should not be sold as unlocking truth already observed. Protected credentials, reversible fixtures and authorization remain mandatory.

**Economics:** the worst planned request allowance is roughly 60 physical runs in a 30-day period (30 scheduled + 10 analyses + 20 verifications), but these have different depth, bindings and retry costs. Budget using `30 × mean scheduled-run cost + 10 × mean analysis cost + 20 × mean Verify cost + storage/queue/email/hosting`, including confirmation attempts and failures. Use p95 duration/cost for concurrency and reserve planning. The existing `$12` monthly Site cost envelope is a provisional internal target, not measured promotional spend. At 60 equal-cost runs it would leave less than `$0.20` per run after fixed overhead; do not assume that fits. The current readiness projection uses p50 Watch cost × 30, which does not price this entire free offer.

`npm run watch:launch-readiness` on October 10 returned **collecting**, with **0 scheduled executions and no cost projection in the configured database**. That database was not production-attested; zero does not establish that all production monitoring is absent. Its 14-day/100-execution gate remains relevant. Begin with a budgeted canary, prove safety/delivery and actual mean/tail costs, then expand access. If the proposed budget fails, reduce expensive execution volume or scope transparently while keeping results explorable. Do not silently slow an advertised daily schedule. Reserve capacity for existing commitments; admit new Sites only within demonstrated capacity.

### Existing restrictions and first-use mismatches

- `plans.ts`: Free/Pro/Studio have 3/30/90 manual analysis allowances, analysis depths 1/2/3, Site caps 1/5/unlimited in current plan definitions, and different legacy-chat token pools. Most capability families are not separately sold, but depth/volume still make the evaluation unequal.
- `usage.ts`: anonymous allowance is one analysis per seven-day cookie/IP window, not a lifetime limit. Its comment says no AI judge; actual `judgePageStage()` runs triage whenever configured and time permits. `includeAi: false` on anonymous creation chiefly gates later AI review/prescription. Do not use the comment as a capability fact.
- Current Site fix-prompt UI and endpoint accept the authorized provisional session; legacy report capability policy gates prompt bodies to owners/samples. The earlier broad claim that all prompts require signup is incorrect. Preserve tenancy; distinguish these paths before changing gates.
- `run-requests.ts`: user-initiated full-Site reruns consume the allowance; Watch and targeted Verify bypass that counter. The latter still pass through `resolveIncludeAiForNewAudit()`, whose AI decision reads remaining credits. - Free cadence is weekly; Pro/Studio allow daily. `project-watch.ts` schedules enabled confirmed Outcomes, not a blanket promise that every diagnostic area is continuously checked. Provisional Sites begin without Watch. A finished analysis must offer safe setup and show what will actually run next.
- Ownership gates on Verify, monitoring configuration, fixtures, developer keys and private connections are legitimate. API keys/basic MCP and export are already available across authenticated plans; public external-client readiness is a separate gate. Raising everyone's persisted plan to Studio or enabling dev-unlimited would bypass useful commercial and safety boundaries and is rejected.

### Learn dependence and willingness to pay

Measure the journey by attributed cohorts: analysis completed with truthful coverage → category/evidence exploration → Site claimed → monitoring active with confirmed scope → expected runs delivered → meaningful failure investigated → fix handoff → independently verified recovery → monitoring retained after 30/60 days. Existing lifecycle events and durable run/notification records supply much of this; a complete research/cohort view and policy-exposure attribution are not established. Do not use `first_useful_result` as an instruction to show only one result.

Use retained verified responsibility and delivery against the promised schedule as value measures. Dashboard visits are secondary. Track stale/paused scope, false positives, repeated Flags, missed executions and failure-to-recovery time alongside conversion. Collect intentional feedback from quiet healthy users as well as users with failures; do not create alerts to simulate value.

Test the planned price after users experience ongoing responsibility: ask which Site they would continue covering at $49/month, what they would stop using instead, and what would make them decline. Record price-specific continuation interest separately from a generic waitlist. Compare stated intent with actual retained monitoring and, after registration/payments/approval, voluntary paid conversion. No fake checkout, card collection, manufactured urgency, or automatic charge. Until that final behavior is observed, willingness to pay remains unproven.

## Synthesis and first coherent slice

The opportunity is to make a substantial monitoring system easy to adopt and trust for commercially important websites. We may be underestimating competitors' sophistication and overestimating what a reassuring headline proves. We also cannot treat all analysis breadth as continuously monitored scope.

Simplify the customer's decisions, not the available evidence. Elevate successful verification, useful failures, scope and the next promised run. Stop narrow teaser optimization, unsupported novelty claims, repeated monitoring prose and decorative integration storytelling. Preserve the URL entry, shared board, independent engine, protected-action safety, stable history and broad diagnostics. The advantage to earn is less work to establish coverage and reach a trustworthy answer, across an ongoing relationship; measure this directly with prospective customers using alternatives.

**First slice:** the homepage URL entry and an explorable, comprehensive shared Site sample. Correct shared hierarchy/mobile reflow; include evidenced healthy areas, failures and meaningful unknown coverage; give every category useful depth; keep individual Flag scope coherent; and show analysis versus monitoring as distinct states. Keep real Analyze working. This advances the selected value proposition and tests comprehension without a whole-site rewrite or entitlement change. It supersedes the earlier recommendation to treat card polish alone as the first strategic slice.

Owner decisions needed: recommended positioning/initial customer; headline/support; comprehensive sample scope and monitoring representation; removal/relegation of the matrix/orbit sections; and the temporary grant's duration, execution budgets and rollout criteria. Numeric access changes are a separate implementation with billing/authorization tests and production evidence. No proposed decision has been silently adopted.
