## Quick start

Paste the public URL of the product you want to review into FixFlags. The first result ranks what matters across Message, Experience, and Reach, then gives you one clear place to begin.

1. Run a check from the homepage.
2. Open the highest-ranked Flag and inspect its evidence.
3. Copy the fix prompt into your editor, or connect the editor through MCP.
4. Deploy the change and run a Re-check.

[Run your first check](/docs/getting-started) or [connect your editor](/docs/integrations#quick-start).

## The product loop

FixFlags follows one loop: **Check → Fix → Re-check → Watch**.

- **Check** the live product the way a user experiences it.
- **Fix** the highest-impact Flag with an evidence-backed prompt.
- **Re-check** after deployment to confirm what changed.
- **Watch** the product over time when monitoring is available on your plan.
