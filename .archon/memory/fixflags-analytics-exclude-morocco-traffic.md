# FixFlags analytics: exclude Morocco traffic

When reporting FixFlags GA4/GSC (or any product traffic metrics), filter out Morocco (MA) traffic from totals. Morocco sessions are mainly founder development traffic, not real users. Always state when Morocco is excluded.
