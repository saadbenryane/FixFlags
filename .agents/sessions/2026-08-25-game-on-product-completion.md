# Game On product completion

## Outcome

Complete the shipped Product Review → Fix → Verify → Watch wedge under one honest homepage, report, access, Improvement, verification, and release contract.

## Execution waves

Wave 1 assigns exclusive scopes for homepage information architecture, report workspace/access presentation, and Product-loop services.
Root owns integration, tracking files, cross-lane contracts, and final proof.

Wave 2 begins only after Wave 1 focused gates pass.
It covers release orchestration, integration verification, terminology, canonical documentation, and proven dead-code removal.

## Anti-cheat constraints

- Do not weaken anonymous server redaction to satisfy UI tests.
- Do not infer verified Improvement from a missing Flag.
- Do not allow manual Update reviews before authentication or outside the shared Product Review meter.
- Do not add a fallback report, compatibility route, feature flag, fake sample, or synthetic production receipt.
- Do not skip a credentialed or conditional release journey and still report completion.
- Do not deploy or publish from a dirty tree, failed CI, mixed SHA, or mixed release/production origin.
