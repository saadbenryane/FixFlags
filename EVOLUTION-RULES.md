# Evolution rules

## Authority and versioning

The owner's latest explicit direction wins. The accepted product vision is [knowledge/vision.md](knowledge/vision.md), revised 2026-09-08. Customer objects and navigation are [docs/product-architecture.md](docs/product-architecture.md). Customer-facing vocabulary and voice are [docs/voice-and-copy.md](docs/voice-and-copy.md). Sequence is [docs/product-masterplan.md](docs/product-masterplan.md). The report, three-rubric presentation, Finish Plan, AI-builder-only positioning and Shopify-only company bet are not permanent product invariants.

Git history preserves earlier documents. Do not append an override above pages of contradictory active guidance. Replace the superseded guidance, update indexes/skills/roadmap, and retain only clearly scoped historical or compatibility facts.

## Change procedure

1. Identify the fact's home in [CANONICAL-SOURCES.md](CANONICAL-SOURCES.md).
2. Mark whether the change is VISION, NEXT or proven SHIPPED.
3. Update that home and its relevant consumers. Prefer pointers over parallel specifications.
4. Record consequential decisions and a phased migration when existing data, access or behavior differs.
5. Validate links, related skills and documentation consistency. Application changes also need relevant tests and real-path proof.
6. Record the changed scope and evidence in the task session and board.

## Preserve foundations, replace experience

The new Site experience is authorized direction. Preserve brand identity, security, billing records, ownership, independent verification and useful evidence infrastructure. These are reasons to plan adapters, not reasons to retain the old report interface.

Current APIs, stored identifiers, rubrics, quotas and tests may remain temporarily for compatibility. Scope them to the old implementation. Change them only with deliberate migration and regression evidence. Never present a partial UI rename as completion of the vision.

## Claims and examples

Source examples do not establish current counts, monitoring cadence, integrations, prices, guarantees or capabilities. Public language must match the actually released behavior. The full product may stay in VISION while a specific slice moves from NEXT to SHIPPED.

No invented revenue-loss estimate, causal attribution, alert success, verified recovery or healthy state. Evidence and coverage govern such claims.
