Features

Accessibility Auditing

Checks every frame for WCAG 2.1 AA compliance: contrast ratios, tap target sizes, heading hierarchy, and screen reader labels. Get a prioritized list of issues with exact fix locations.

Design System Enforcement

Upload your design tokens and Forma validates every component against your system. Spot off-brand colors, custom spacing values, and rogue font sizes across hundreds of frames.

Instant Reports

Run a full review in under 30 seconds. Results appear as annotations directly in Figma, with severity levels and one-click fix suggestions.
