# JTBD Research for FixFlags

Use when validating Segment B (existing site owners), writing FAQ/objection copy, or running customer interviews.

## Switch interview (15–20 min)

Interview people who **recently ran an audit or upgraded**. Do not ask about features first.

| Phase | Question | What you're mining |
|-------|----------|-------------------|
| Trigger | "What happened on the day you decided to audit your site?" | Push force, segment fit |
| Struggle | "What were you trying to fix before you found us?" | Verbatim pain for headlines |
| Alternatives | "What did you try instead? Lighthouse, agency, ChatGPT?" | Competitive copy, habit force |
| Anxiety | "What almost stopped you from pasting your URL?" | Trust line, FAQ, boundaries |
| Outcome | "What's different now that you have the report?" | Proof lines, re-check upsell |
| Hire/fire | "When would you run another audit?" | Trigger calendar (launch, ads, redesign) |

Capture **exact phrases**. Mirror them in copy before polishing.

## Four Forces → copy placement

| Force | Segment A example | Segment B example | Where it lives |
|-------|-------------------|-------------------|----------------|
| **Push** | "Scared to share the link" | "Traffic but not enough signups" | Problem section, ad hook |
| **Pull** | "Fix prompts in Cursor in one paste" | "Prioritized list in 60 seconds" | Hero subhead, workflow |
| **Anxiety** | "Is this just another score?" | "Do I need an agency after this?" | Trust line, FAQ, sample report CTA |
| **Habit** | "Lighthouse said 90" | "Homepage looks fine to us" | Differentiation table, FAQ[0] |

Anxiety copy must **reduce** risk, not hype the product. Free first audit, sample output, and explicit disqualifiers beat superlatives.

## JTBD content audit checklist

Apply to any page before shipping copy:

- [ ] Headline states customer struggle or outcome, not product category
- [ ] Copy uses customer verbs (paste, fix, re-check, hand off) not adjectives (comprehensive, robust)
- [ ] FAQ answers job-related concerns (Lighthouse enough? live site? agency?) not only features
- [ ] Proof shows transformation (graded finding → fix → re-check), not feature list
- [ ] CTA names the next step outcome ("Audit my site" = see your grades)
- [ ] Emotional outcome acknowledged once (confidence, relief from guessing)

## Segment variant map (same product, different job)

When one page serves two jobs, split by **section**, not **sentence**:

| Section | AI shipper job | Existing site owner job |
|---------|----------------|-------------------------|
| Hero | One primary only | One primary only |
| Problem | Ship fast, miss details | Live site, flat conversion |
| Workflow | Paste → fix in editor → re-check | Paste → prioritize → hand off or fix → re-check |
| Social proof | Strong sites fail at launch | Polished sites miss conversion gaps |
| FAQ | Lovable/Bolt, MCP | Lighthouse vs conversion, live URL |

## Research without interviews

Proxy method when you have no calls yet:

1. Run 5 public audits on live SaaS homepages (yours or ICP examples)
2. List top findings in Conversion, Trust, Mobile
3. For each finding, write the **owner's worry** in first person ("We get traffic but the hero doesn't say what to do")
4. Check if FixFlags finding text already mirrors that worry
5. Gap → draft headline or FAQ from the worry, not from product internals

Store verbatim phrases in segment experiment brief (main SKILL.md template).
